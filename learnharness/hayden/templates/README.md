# Starting templates

Paste a starter as your first message in a **new session** (teaching or practice). The length is a preference the tutor scopes to, not a timer. The profile must be on (`npx tsx hayden/profile.ts on`) for the length cue to mean anything beyond plain text.

Each template has **held-out transfer items** in [`held-out-items.md`](held-out-items.md). Don't read them before the session; they're for the 7-day check in [`../evals/retention-protocol.md`](../evals/retention-protocol.md).

## 1 · Medical physics: the linear-quadratic model and fractionation

- **Short (~10 min):** `Short session (~10 min). Goal: what α/β actually means in the linear-quadratic model, not just its typical values.`
- **Normal (~25 min):** `Normal session (~25 min). Goal: why fractionation spares late-responding tissue relative to tumour under the LQ model: what α/β means, the assumptions behind BED, and where LQ is contested.`
- **Deep (~45 min):** `Deep session (~45 min). Goal: derive BED and EQD2 from the LQ survival curve, state every assumption (full repair between fractions, no repopulation), and work out where the model is questioned at large doses per fraction.`

## 2 · Statistics / AI: cross-entropy is maximum likelihood

- **Short (~10 min):** `Short session (~10 min). Goal: why minimizing cross-entropy for a binary classifier is maximum-likelihood estimation.`
- **Normal (~25 min):** `Normal session (~25 min). Goal: why cross-entropy loss is maximum likelihood, what distributional assumption that encodes, and what it does and does not imply about calibration.`
- **Deep (~45 min):** `Deep session (~45 min). Goal: the general recipe "choose a noise model → negative log-likelihood → loss", applied to Bernoulli, Gaussian, and Laplace noise, plus why accuracy and calibration are different properties.`

## 3 · Business: subscription unit economics

- **Short (~10 min):** `Short session (~10 min). Goal: what CAC payback measures and what it ignores.`
- **Normal (~25 min):** `Normal session (~25 min). Goal: contribution margin, CAC, payback period, and LTV for a subscription product, and how they connect.`
- **Deep (~45 min):** `Deep session (~45 min). Goal: unit economics end to end (contribution margin, CAC, payback, LTV, LTV:CAC), the retention model hidden inside the LTV formula, and why LTV is fragile to churn assumptions.`

All examples are synthetic. Nothing in a session is patient-specific clinical advice.
